## Interpretation

The first CUDA kernel set is correct and useful in the intended resident-data
regimes. Tiny operations remain CPU-faster because launch and transfer overhead
dominate. Medium deterministic workloads on H100 show CUDA wins for
transfer-inclusive and device-resident simplify, statevector expectation,
pairwise commutation, and matrix-product generation followed by simplify. The
refreshed pairwise commutation case also confirms that the CUDA device-resident
path is faster than both available optimized CPU selectors on this host.

These measurements are source-build evidence on one H100 host. They are not
CUDA wheel distribution claims, and they should not be generalized to other GPU
architectures without the hardware-target evidence required by
`docs/architecture/hardware_targets_and_testing.md`.

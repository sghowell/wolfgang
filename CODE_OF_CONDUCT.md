# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Wolfgang a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical community.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise and respectful, especially when reviewing correctness or performance claims.
- Critique code, evidence, and ideas—not people.
- State uncertainty and hardware/tooling limitations honestly.
- Welcome reproducible counter-evidence and correction.
- Remove credentials, personal paths, host identifiers, and private infrastructure details from reports.
- Respect differing levels of systems, quantum-computing, and accelerator experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and knowingly fabricated technical evidence.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `sean.g.howell@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, issue warnings, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.
